About the shop

A collaborative space where classroom concepts become tangible systems.

What students do

Students work through the full engineering cycle: identify a problem, design a solution, build a prototype, test the result, and communicate what they learned.

Projects can include robotic arms, conveyor systems, mobile robots, pneumatic devices, sensor networks, and simulated smart-factory cells.

Core skills

  • Electrical safety and wiring
  • PLC logic and HMI concepts
  • CAD and mechanical assembly
  • Robotics and motion control
  • Measurement and troubleshooting
  • Team communication
